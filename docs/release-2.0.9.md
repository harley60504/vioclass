# VioClass 2.0.9

## 繁體中文

- 直播暫停後繼續播放會重新接回最新直播；DVR、影片與剪輯保留觀看位置。
- 畫質按鈕整合畫質與 0.5～2× 播放速度，新增最高畫質與自動畫質模式。
- 播放器設定從影片右側展開，直接調整手勢、影像與音訊設定。
- 畫質、倍速與音量選單靠近各自的按鈕展開，與設定面板統一採半透明深色樣式。
- 過暗的聊天室名字會保留原色相並提高亮度，改善黑色、深藍等名字的可讀性。

## English

- Resuming a paused live stream reconnects to the live edge. DVR, videos, and clips keep their viewing position.
- The quality button now opens a quick menu for quality and 0.5–2× playback speed, including highest-quality and automatic-quality modes.
- Player settings slide in from the video's right edge, with gesture, video, and audio preferences in one panel.
- Quality, speed, and volume menus open beside their controls and share a translucent dark style with the settings panel.
- Dark chat name colors are brightened while preserving their hue for better readability.
