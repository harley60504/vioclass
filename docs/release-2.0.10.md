# VioClass 2.0.10

## 繁體中文

- 音量按鈕移至播放器左側、播放按鈕旁邊。
- 播放器設定整合至畫質快速選單，減少控制列按鈕。
- 修正畫質選單捲動與 About 切頁衝突，選單捲到底也不會意外切頁。
- 改善退出全螢幕後上方返回、主頁與視窗控制列的狀態同步。

## English

- Moved volume to the left side of the player, next to play/pause.
- Integrated player settings into the quality quick menu to simplify the control bar.
- Fixed scrolling in the quality menu triggering navigation to About, including at the end of the menu.
- Improved synchronization of the back, home, and window controls after leaving fullscreen.
